Welcome to The Grand Seaside Hotel! We are delighted to host you. Here is some important information to make your stay comfortable.

Check-in time is 3:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. If you need a late check-out, please contact the front desk, and we will do our best to accommodate your request depending on availability.

Our complimentary breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM in the Ocean View Dining Room on the first floor.

The hotel features a heated indoor pool and a state-of-the-art fitness center, both located on the basement level. These facilities are open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Towels are provided at the pool.

For your convenience, we offer complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hotel. The network name is 'Seaside_Guest' and the password is 'oceanbreeze2024'.

Room service is available 24/7. You can find the menu on the digital tablet provided in your room, or you can call extension 400 to place an order.

If you are looking to explore the area, we highly recommend visiting the historic lighthouse, which is just a 15-minute walk from the hotel. There are also several excellent seafood restaurants along the boardwalk; our concierge can help you make a reservation.

We provide a shuttle service to and from the airport. It runs every hour on the hour between 5:00 AM and 11:00 PM. Please book your spot at the front desk at least 12 hours in advance.

Pets are welcome at our hotel for a non-refundable fee of $50 per stay. We provide pet beds and bowls upon request. Please ensure your pets are on a leash in public areas.

If you experience any issues with your room, such as air conditioning problems or require extra pillows, please dial 0 from your room phone to reach the front desk immediately.
